Getting Started

MHS Findr helps students and teachers manage lost and found items in a safe and organized way. All posts and claims are reviewed by teachers to ensure accuracy and security.

Frequently Asked Questions



Click Sign Up, enter your school email, create a password, select your role, and confirm your email to activate your account.

Go to the Report page, fill out the item details, optionally upload a photo, and submit for teacher approval.

Browse approved items, click Claim, and provide proof of ownership. Teachers will review and approve or reject your claim.

Teacher approval ensures safety, prevents false claims, and keeps the platform trustworthy for all students.

Contact a teacher or school administrator who manages MHS Findr. They can assist with account, item, or claim issues.
